What This Means For You

Liver Qi Stagnation occurs when emotional stress or frustration causes your Liver's energy to become "stuck." This affects the smooth flow of Qi throughout your body, often manifesting as irritability, digestive bloating, and a sense of tension — especially along the sides of your body.

The Liver governs the free flow of Qi and emotions. When stagnated, it can also affect the Spleen (digestion), explaining your bloating symptoms.

逍遙散

Xiāo Yáo Sǎn

Why This Formula?

Xiao Yao San is the classic formula for Liver Qi Stagnation with Spleen involvement. It soothes the Liver, strengthens the Spleen, and nourishes Blood — addressing both your primary and secondary patterns simultaneously.

Ingredients

柴胡 Chai Hu (Bupleurum)
Chief 9g

Radix Bupleuri — Spreads Liver Qi, relieves stagnation, and lifts the Yang Qi. The primary herb for soothing the Liver.

當歸 Dang Gui (Angelica)
Deputy 9g
白芍 Bai Shao (White Peony)
Deputy 9g
白朮 Bai Zhu (Atractylodes)
Assistant 9g
茯苓 Fu Ling (Poria)
Assistant 9g
甘草 Gan Cao (Licorice)
Envoy 4.5g

Preparation

1

Soak herbs in 3 cups of cold water for 30 minutes.

2

Bring to a boil, then simmer on low heat for 25–30 minutes until reduced to 1 cup.

3

Strain and drink warm. Can be re-decocted once with 2 cups water.

Take 30 minutes before meals, twice daily (AM & PM)

Dosage Guidance

Standard adult dose: 1 cup (200ml) twice daily. Adjust for body weight — reduce by 20% for adults under 50kg. Course: 2–4 weeks, then reassess.

Safety Check

Drug Interaction Detected

Gan Cao (Licorice) may interact with Metformin. Monitor blood glucose levels closely. Consult your prescribing physician.

No Allergy Conflicts

None of the ingredients match your listed allergies.

Contraindication Note

Not recommended during pregnancy. Discontinue if experiencing significant heat symptoms.

Screen-by-Screen Dev Notes

1. Onboarding

  • Horizontal paged scroll (UIPageViewController / ViewPager2)
  • Yin-Yang SVG: use CSS rotate animation, 20s duration
  • Five Elements: staggered scale-in on slide entry
  • Qi particles: SVG <animateMotion> or Lottie
  • Store "onboarding_completed" flag in local storage

2. Profile Setup

  • Tag input: autocomplete search from allergy database
  • Photo upload: camera intent for medication photos
  • Constitution quiz: 3 questions → scoring matrix → Yang Deficiency / Yin Deficiency / Qi Deficiency / Balanced
  • Persist to local SQLite / Realm database

3. Home Dashboard

  • Greeting dynamically set: morning/afternoon/evening based on device time
  • Constitution card pulls latest diagnosis result
  • Seasonal tip: map current date to TCM season (Spring=Wood, Summer=Fire, Late Summer=Earth, Autumn=Metal, Winter=Water)
  • Bottom nav: 5-item tab bar, FAB-less design

4. Symptom Wizard

  • 4-step wizard with back-preserving navigation state
  • Step 2 is dynamic: generates symptom detail cards based on Step 1 selections
  • Tongue photo: integrate ML model (TFLite/CoreML) for classification
  • Pulse cards: illustrative only — no sensor integration
  • All selections persisted in memory until submission

5. Diagnosis Result

  • Pattern engine: weighted symptom scoring against 50+ TCM patterns
  • Confidence: Strong (>75%), Moderate (50-75%), Mild (<50%)
  • Body map: SVG with programmatic organ highlighting
  • Five Elements color-coding: Wood=#4A7C2E, Fire=#C44B2B, Earth=#C8A96E, Metal=#8B8B8B, Water=#2B5EA7

6. Formula Detail

  • Ingredient cards: expandable accordion pattern
  • Roles: Chief (君), Deputy (臣), Assistant (佐), Envoy (使)
  • Safety checker: cross-reference user.medications against herb-drug interaction DB
  • Allergy check: cross-reference user.allergies against ingredient properties
  • Preparation tabs: segmented control, content swap

7. History & Tracking

  • Timeline: reverse-chronological, lazy-loaded
  • Trend chart: use lightweight charting lib (e.g., Victory Native, MPAndroidChart)
  • Comparison mode: side-by-side scroll with synced highlighting
  • PDF export: generate on-device using template + data injection

8. Disclaimer

  • Modal overlay — blocks all interaction until acknowledged
  • Shown once before first diagnosis, gate stored as boolean flag
  • Checkbox required to enable "Continue" button
  • Re-accessible from Settings > Legal at any time

Accessibility Requirements

  • Minimum 16px body text (already enforced in design tokens)
  • All color pairs meet WCAG AA contrast ratio (4.5:1 minimum)
  • All interactive elements have minimum 44x44pt touch targets
  • VoiceOver / TalkBack: all images have descriptive alt text
  • Semantic heading hierarchy maintained per screen
  • Focus order follows visual layout (top-to-bottom, left-to-right)
  • Motion: respect "Reduce Motion" system setting — disable Qi animations
  • Dynamic Type / font scaling support up to 200%
